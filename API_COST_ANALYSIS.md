# API & Privacy Strategy

You asked: *"I think there are free API keys for this, or do I need paid models for this?"*

### The Verdict:
**You can build 100% of this for free right now.**

### 1. The Free Route (Google Gemini API)
Google offers a generous free tier for the Gemini API.
*   **Cost:** $0.
*   **Limits:** 15 Requests per minute (Plenty for a single user).
*   **Privacy Caveat:** In the *free tier*, Google reserves the right to use the inputs and outputs to tune their models.
*   **Conclusion:** Perfect for building the prototype and testing the logic.

### 2. The Privacy Route (Paid API)
Since this is a **Personal Journal** containing your deepest thoughts, you will eventually want strict privacy.
*   **Model:** Gemini 1.5 Flash.
*   **Cost:** ~$0.075 per 1 million input tokens.
    *   *Real world math:* You could write a novel's worth of journal entries every month and pay less than **$1.00 USD**.
*   **Privacy:** When you put a credit card on file (Pay-as-you-go), Google **does not** use your data for training. It is strictly confidential.

### 3. Recommendation
1.  Develop the app using the **Free Key**.
2.  Once you are happy with it and start putting real, sensitive life data into it, switch to a **Paid Key** for the peace of mind. It is cheaper than a cup of coffee per month.
